## Description

Give your thoughts room to move.

InfinityPaper is a quiet drawing space for quick sketches, handwritten ideas, diagrams, and doodles. Let the paper move as you draw, or lock its movement when you want more control.

DRAW YOUR WAY
Choose colors, stroke widths, and solid, dashed, or dotted lines. On a compatible iPad, use Apple Pencil with pressure and tilt settings, or enable Pencil-only drawing.

REARRANGE YOUR IDEAS
Loop around strokes with lasso selection, then move, duplicate, or delete them. Use the whole-stroke eraser and undo or redo as you work.

KEEP YOUR WORK TOGETHER
Save separate drawings in your library. Rename, duplicate, and revisit them whenever an idea needs another look.

ADD A REFERENCE
Import an image from Photos or Files. Adjust its size, opacity, visibility, and position, then draw alongside or over it.

MAKE SPACE TO THINK
Choose plain, lined, grid, or dotted paper. Pan and zoom around your work, fit the drawing to the screen, or hide the tools in Focus mode.

SHARE THE RESULT
Export the visible area or the full drawing as PDF or PNG, with options for the reference image, background, and margins.

Your drawings are stored locally on your device. No account is required.

## App Review notes

No sign-in, test account, subscription, or purchase is required. The app works offline.

Create or open a drawing from My drawings. The top drawing-tool menu contains Pen, Whole-stroke eraser, and Lasso selection. With Lasso selection, loop around strokes and drag inside the dashed frame to move them; reopen the tool menu for duplicate/delete actions.

The line-width button opens the line style and width grids. The color button opens the color grid.

Settings → Reference image imports one image per drawing through the system Photos or Files picker. Changes are applied with Done. Settings also contains How to use InfinityPaper and Privacy policy.

On iPad, Apple Pencil settings are in Settings. Pressure and tilt behavior requires compatible hardware. In Pencil-only mode, one finger pans; otherwise two fingers pan. Pinch zoom and two-finger double-tap reset are supported.

The More (…) menu includes My drawings, view controls, and Focus mode. Show tools exits Focus mode. Export shares PDF or PNG through the system share sheet.
